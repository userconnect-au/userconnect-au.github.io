Safety

A community agreement everyone signs, and checks that keep it.

The community agreement

Everyone agrees to this when they join:

  1. Be kind. Everyone here is at a different stage, and all of them are welcome.
  2. No buying, selling, sharing or sourcing drugs. No dosing advice.
  3. Stay anonymous. Don't share real names or home addresses, and think carefully before sharing a phone number or your username on another app.
  4. We're peer support, not a crisis service. Help is always at the top.

How it's kept safe

Every message checked

Every message is checked for safety before anyone sees it. Buying, selling or sourcing drugs is blocked.

Help when it's needed

If a message sounds like someone is in crisis, it's still sent, and they're shown people to talk to straight away.

Block and report

Block or report anyone. Reports that someone is in danger or under 18 are dealt with urgently.

Fair decisions

Breaking the agreement leads to a warning, then a mute, a pause or removal. Anyone can ask for a decision to be looked at again, and an independent arbitrator checks decisions.

Your privacy

We never ask for your real name, email or phone number. Members' data is stored in Australia (Sydney), and you choose who sees your drug use and mental health choices. Read the Privacy Policy.
