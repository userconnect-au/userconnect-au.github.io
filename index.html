Why people use Undrcurrent

Find people who get it

We match you on shared interests, drug use and mental health, so conversations start from common ground.

Stay anonymous

No real name, email or phone number. You decide who sees what.

Feel less alone, day or night

One-to-one chats, small group rooms, and peer supporters who have been there too.

Safe by design

Every message is checked for safety, drug dealing is blocked, and Help is one tap away.

Getting started takes a few minutes

  1. Create an anonymous account with a username and password.
  2. Set up your profile using the three choices: Interest, Drug use and Mental health, and say if you'd like peer support.
  3. See your matches on the Search page and say hi.
  4. Join rooms on topics that matter to you.

More about how it works

Talk to Mae, any time

Rather not talk to a person right now? Mae is always there to listen and encourage you. Mae is automatic, not a real person, and she'll point you to help if you need it.

Safe by design

  • Every message is checked for safety before anyone sees it.
  • Buying, selling or sourcing drugs is blocked. Talking about drug use, recovery and harm reduction is always welcome.
  • Block or report anyone. Anyone can ask for a decision to be looked at again.

How we keep it safe

Need help right now?

If you are in danger or someone may have overdosed, call your emergency number now. If you have naloxone, use it and stay with them.
